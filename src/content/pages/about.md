I’m Harisankar. I build products and the systems that keep them running.

My interests run from Linux and PC building to software, AI tools, and the everyday work of running a business. I enjoy taking a practical problem and finding a useful way through it — sometimes that means code, sometimes hardware, and sometimes a better workflow.

## What I build

[TipKaro](/projects/tipkaro/) brings together creator payments and stream workflows. [Callery](/projects/callery/) makes call recordings easier to organize and find. [Hermes Phone](/projects/hermes-phone/) connects a conversational assistant to a regular mobile phone.

They’re different kinds of products, but each starts with something people do and asks how it could work better.

## Beyond the projects

This site is also a place for notes, experiments, and things that catch my attention. I don’t want to pin the writing to a fixed set of topics; it will follow what I’m working on and curious about.
