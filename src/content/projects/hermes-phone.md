## An assistant on a regular phone

Hermes Phone connects a regular mobile phone to Hermes Agent over Bluetooth. It explores a familiar way to interact with a conversational assistant: through a handset.

## Connecting software and hardware

The project brings the assistant and the phone together, with the handset as the interface for conversation.
